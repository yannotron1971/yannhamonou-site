## Positions

Mean longitudes and rates from JPL, Approximate Positions of the Planets,
Table 1 (1800 AD to 2050 AD), https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/planets/approx_pos.html.
The page places each planet on a circular orbit at its mean longitude.
