Reference study · edge-concept

Five moves the Edge has already earned.

The Edge already runs one near-black ground, one enormous light line and mono micro-labels. Nothing below proposes changing that. Each reference is a site that pushed one of those three decisions further than this build currently does — shown so you can judge how far you want to go, not as a template to match.

Source · Inspo archive Brief · dark / serious / monochrome 24 sites measured No changes applied

The archive's gravity runs against you. Stay against it.

Across 24 sites matching this brief, 83% use a grotesk display face — the Edge does too. But the same measurement puts the accent hue consensus at chromatic-other, 42%, with only 4% landing on neutral. Almost everything in this category eventually reaches for a colour.

The Edge's DESIGN.md rules that out on purpose: a page built on one light line and mono labels can give every section its own ground and still read as one material; colour would break it. That is the single most distinctive decision in the build, and the archive is evidence it's rare rather than evidence it's wrong.

So the ranking below contains no palette advice. Every move is about scale, position and restraint — the three axes still open to you once hue is off the table.

Ranked — most to least worth doing

01

Let the index become the fold.

Adcker — “Index” · recommended

Adcker's navigation is the hero: six stacked all-caps lines at roughly 120px, zero leading, letters nearly touching between rows — with 12px labels flanking each line left and right (“Home”, “What we do”, “Projects”, “Showreel”). Scale jump measured at 10:1. No CTA anywhere in the fold.

You already have this grammar — the Services section runs numbered index rows (06 Marketing Leadership) with a mono descriptor to the right. Adcker is the same structure at ten times the size, promoted from a mid-page section to the opening statement.

What transfersThe flanking-label pattern: a big line with small mono text on both margins. It gives a row two reading speeds at once, which is exactly what your services rows are already trying to do with number + name + descriptor.

02

Take the headline out and let the corners carry it.

Aristide Benoist · aristidebenoist.com · captured 4 May 2026

The fold has no headline at all. Wordmark top-left, one word top-right, a fragmented visual centre-right, and micro-text pinned to the bottom corners: INDEPENDENT DEVELOPER / AVAILABLE APR. 2023 bottom-left, a contact stack bottom-right. Logo runs ~24px, body ~8px — an extreme scale jump in the opposite direction from Adcker.

Your hero already does a version of this: STONEHAVEN · ABERDEENSHIRE · SCOTLAND sits on the baseline opposite the CTA. This is the end state of that idea — the corners hold the facts, and the centre holds only the field.

What transfersCorner anchoring as a system, not a flourish. Availability, location and contact all become baseline furniture, which frees the centre of the hero to be nothing but the moving ground.

03

Crop enormous type against the bottom edge as structure.

Monolog · bymonolog.com · captured 10 Sep 2026

A small centred copy block floats in negative space, and the bottom 40% of the viewport is filled by the wordmark set gigantic and cropped hard at both edges. It reads as a structural footer to the first screen rather than as decoration — the type is load-bearing.

Take the composition, not the weight. Monolog's face is heavy, wide Khteka and its ground measures L 40 — mid grey, not dark. The Edge runs #151515 under an enormous light-weight line. This is the closest reference here on mood and the furthest on execution; adopting its type weight would undo your own distinction.

What transfersCropping display type at the viewport edge so it behaves as architecture. At 200 weight rather than 800, the same move reads quiet instead of loud.

04

Keep the interface hueless; let one image hold all the colour.

Palais de Tokyo

Pure black ground, pure white type, a monochrome interface throughout — nav, headings, listings, all neutral. The only colour on the page is inside a single circular image: a rust-toned specimen photograph that reads as a laboratory sample dropped into a museum wall.

This is a precedent for a rule adjacent to yours. Your no-hue rule governs the system; it does not have to govern photography. A client logo, a portrait, a single plate could carry colour without any token changing — the ground ladder stays hueless and the page still reads as one material.

What transfersPermission, narrowly drawn. Colour confined to content, never to chrome — so the rule in DESIGN.md holds exactly as written while the page gains one warm point of fixation.

05

The contrast case: what luxe-dark looks like when it reaches for a serif.

Origin — About · for comparison, not adoption

Same near-black ground, same restraint, same category read — but the display voice is an italic roman serif, and the whole page tips from technical to wealth management. It is the most direct evidence available for why your grotesk choice is doing work.

Included so the ranking has a floor. If anyone argues the Edge would feel more premium with a serif line, this is what that actually buys and costs.

What transfersNothing — deliberately. Use it as the control against moves 01–03.

Read the references with these in hand

The palette swatches are measurements, not proposals.

Inspo returns a palette for every capture, and it returned Raycast's crimson as a suggestion for this brief. That is a description of Raycast, not advice for the Edge. Every thumbnail on this page is desaturated for exactly that reason.

These are real shipped sites, not compliant ones.

Several would fail a strict contrast or motion audit. Your DESIGN.md already carries measured ratios for every ink level against every ground — that is stricter than most of what is pictured here, and worth keeping.

Fold discipline still applies.

The archive's measured guidance: nav, headline, supporting line and CTA complete inside 1280 × 800, display type sized to two or three balanced lines. Moves 01 and 03 both enlarge type — check the fold after either.

One rhythm at every seam.

Measured median step between sections across the set: 96px, with 80–160px the working band. Below ~64px two sections read as one block. Your ground ladder changes background per section, which makes a consistent step more important, not less.
